Words in two languages:
look up, collect, compete

VOQUIZ is a Chrome extension for students who learn in both Korean and English. Look up a word in either language and get an explanation at your grade level in both, then practise with quizzes made from your own words.

What you can do

The words you look up become what you study.

Dictionary

Search in Korean or English and see the meaning, word origin, a memory tip and examples in both languages.

Word list

Every word you look up is kept by day. Mark favourites and words to review.

Quizzes and streaks

Quiz yourself on your own words, or keep a streak going on the recommended words for your grade.

Duels with friends

Three minutes, the same questions: see who gets more right.

84 characters to collect

Earn points by looking up words and answering quizzes, and spend them on characters and themes.

How to use

  1. Install VOQUIZ in Chrome and sign in with your Google account.
  2. The first time, enter the invite code a friend gave you.
  3. Look up a word. You can also select a word on any web page and right-click.

Use of Google account data

VOQUIZ uses Google Sign-In only to receive your email address and basic profile (account identifier), so that your word list and records follow you across computers. It does not access contacts, mail, Drive or any other Google data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
