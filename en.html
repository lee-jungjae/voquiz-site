Words in two languages:
look up, collect, compete

VOQUIZ is a Chrome extension for students in grades 3–12 who learn in both Korean and English. Look up a word in either language and get an explanation at your grade level in both, then practise with quizzes made from your own words.

Dictionary

Search in Korean or English and see the meaning, word origin, a memory tip and examples in both languages.

Word list

Every word you look up is kept by day. Mark favourites and words to review.

Quizzes

Quizzes from your own words, a streak challenge on recommended words for each grade, and three-minute duels with friends.

Characters and points

Earn points by looking up words and answering quizzes, and spend them on characters and themes.

How to use

  • Install VOQUIZ in Chrome and sign in with your Google account.
  • To join for the first time you need an invite code from a friend.
  • You can also select a word on any web page and right-click to look it up.

Use of Google account data

VOQUIZ uses Google Sign-In only to receive your email address and basic profile (account identifier), so that your word list and records follow you across computers. It does not access contacts, mail, Drive or any other Google data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
